Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

EarnIQ ("we", "us", or "our") is a mobile application for automotive sales professionals to track deals and commissions. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is processed and stored, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

EarnIQ does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use the app. The following data is stored locally on your device only using AsyncStorage and is never uploaded to our servers:

  • Deal records you create (vehicle details, gross profit, commission amounts, dates, notes)
  • Spiff/bonus records (description, amount, date)
  • Pay plan configuration (commission tiers, caps, bonuses)
  • Monthly goals (unit and commission targets)
  • App preferences and onboarding status

If you delete the app, all locally stored data is permanently removed from your device.

2. Deal Sheet Scanning (AI-Assisted Import)

EarnIQ includes an optional feature that lets you photograph or import a PDF of a deal sheet. When you use this feature:

  • The image or PDF is transmitted to our processing server solely to extract deal fields (vehicle name, gross profits, date, and deal type).
  • The server forwards the image or extracted text to OpenAI's API to perform the extraction.
  • The extracted fields are returned to your device and pre-filled in the deal entry form.
  • Neither we nor OpenAI store the image or PDF after processing. The data is used only to fulfill your immediate request and is not retained, used for training, or shared further.

This feature requires camera or photo library access on your device, which you must grant via the system permission prompt. You can decline or revoke that permission at any time in your device settings without affecting other app features.

For more information on how OpenAI handles data, see openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.

3. Data Shared via Export

EarnIQ allows you to export your deal data as a CSV file using your device's native share sheet (powered by expo-sharing). When you choose to share or export a file, you control where it goes — for example, to your email, a cloud storage app, or a messaging app. We do not receive or have access to any data you choose to share.

4. In-App Purchases (Pro Subscription)

EarnIQ offers an optional Pro subscription that unlocks additional features. Subscriptions are processed through RevenueCat, a third-party payment and subscription management platform. When you make a purchase, RevenueCat may collect:

  • A randomly generated device or user identifier
  • Purchase transaction data (product ID, purchase date, subscription status)
  • Device locale and platform information (iOS/Android)

RevenueCat does not collect your name, email address, or payment card details — those remain with Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). For more information, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Actual payment processing is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment information.

5. What We Do Not Collect

  • No account, email address, or login is required to use EarnIQ.
  • We do not collect your name, location, contacts, or any other personal identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or crash reporting services.
  • Deal records, pay plans, goals, and spiffs are stored on your device only and are never uploaded to our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

EarnIQ is intended for adults (automotive sales professionals) and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of EarnIQ after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@earniq.app.
